Cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game with eleven players on each team at the centre of which is a pitch with two wickets on each end.
The objective of the fielding team is two dismiss the batters and make sure the batters score minimum runs. The objective of the batting team is to score as many runs as possible through either hitting the ball outside boundary or running to the other end of the pitch without dismissal.
Cricket originated in England in the medieval period and it was played by shepherds but later on the aristocrats also took part in it and the game was spread throughout the world through colonization.

The international governing body of cricket is ICC
